Vault & password

Your master password encrypts every private key at rest. Changing it re-seals the vault; the old password stops working immediately, including for backups taken after the change.

Backup & restore

Exports your entire vault - keys (still encrypted), categories, saved servers, known hosts, and settings - as a single sealed file. Restoring requires the master password that was current when the backup was taken.

Backups are sealed with your master password - treat the file as a secret.

Bitwarden / Vaultwarden sync

Keeps your SSH keys mirrored as SSH key items in your own vault (folder SSHSpan by default). Two-way: changes sync in both directions, newest wins; deletions are never propagated automatically. Self-hosted servers must use a public hostname — private, LAN and localhost addresses are refused for safety. The stored vault password is sealed with your SSHSpan master password and only readable while the vault is unlocked.
